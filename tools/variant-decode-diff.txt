[VARIANT_DECODE] (67 chips)
  Variant-decode consolidation (Phase 86 VAR-02) — Rule 1/2/3 replaced by a
    single principled classify(type,proto,pm_idx,flags,pinout).
    The override stack is gone; electrical.type/algorithm/pinout are now derived
    once from the fields minipro itself uses to classify a device. Two effects
    land here as electrical.type-only deltas:
      (a) 68 5V-EEPROM-pinout chips (proto 0x0D, configure_eeprom28c, no VPP)
          decode Flash/EEPROM -> EEPROM. The old two-pass mapped proto 0x0D to
          'Flash/EEPROM'; classify() arm 2 (5V-EEPROM pinout clusters) emits the
          more-accurate 'EEPROM' type for the 28C/28LV/2816 family. NO algorithm,
          pinout, or VPP change — the chips already dispatched to configure_eeprom28c.
      (b) X88C64P (proto 0x34, XICOR NovRAM/EEPROM) decodes UV-EPROM -> EEPROM via
          classify() arm 4b. Display-only: the chip stays
          support_status=protocol-not-implemented and non-dispatchable; algorithm
          (0x34) and pinout unchanged.
    The variant HIGH byte is minipro's T56/T76 algo-file selector, NOT a
    classification axis — classify() keys on type/proto/pm_idx/flags.
    [VERIFIED: minipro database.c#L1918 @ a8efaedc —
     uint8_t algo_number = (uint8_t)(device->variant >> 8) —
     https://gitlab.com/DavidGriffith/minipro/-/blob/a8efaedc/src/database.c#L1918]
    [VERIFIED: minipro minipro.h#L70 MP_SRAM=0x04 —
     https://gitlab.com/DavidGriffith/minipro/-/blob/a8efaedc/src/minipro.h#L70]
    [CITED: tools/DECODE-NOTES.md §2 (high byte) / §4 (X88C64) / §5 (FM1608)]
